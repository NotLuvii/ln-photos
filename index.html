About

LN Photos

I'm an automotive photographer based in New Brunswick, Canada, posting as @ln._photos.

The work follows the local scene: indoor shows like YFC Autofest and Stance East, dealership meets, and one-on-one sessions with owners. Most of it is shot low and wide, often on a fisheye, and much of it happens after the sky turns orange and the streetlights take over.
