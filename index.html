About

Behind the camera.

I shoot cars around New Brunswick, Canada, as LN Photos. Car features are planned with the owner, so we pick the spots and the timing together.

I start with the build, then go looking for light and a setting that suit it, whether that’s a rooftop lot in daylight, low sun through the trees or a gas pump after dark. The details that make a car yours get their own frames too.
