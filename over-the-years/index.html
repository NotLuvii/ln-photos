2022

2022: It started on a phone.

On 1 June 2022 I went to my first car show in Canada. No camera, only my phone, and I got there as people were leaving. Those few minutes got me back into photography, and the account started that week.

2024: Posters.

My goal for the account that year: no more “boring” carousels. Photos started turning into posters and album-cover layouts, the Accord got its own cover, and YFC Autofest 2024 got shot up close.

2025

2025: Starting fresh.

The laptop with all my photos died, so I re-edited what was saved in the cloud and started again. Then a full season: YFC Autofest, Complex Union, the MX-5 from daylight to after dark, and YFC Oktoberfest.
